# Shadow Hunter — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 29 October 2026
**App:** Shadow Hunter: Stealth Ninja (Android package `com.vamsichintada.shadowhunter`)

This policy explains what information Shadow Hunter handles. The short version: **the game does not collect, send or share any personal data.**

## What the app stores

To remember your progress, the game saves a few small values **on your device only**:

| Saved value | Why |
|---|---|
| Gems you have earned | So you can spend them in the shop |
| The furthest level you reached | For the "Continue" button |
| Characters you bought and the one you selected | So your purchases stay unlocked |
| Music and sound effects on/off | So your choice is remembered |

These values never leave your device through the app. They are not linked to your name, email, account or any identifier. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

If you have turned on Android's own backup for your device, Android may include this app's saved values in your device backup, which Google stores in your Google account under Google's privacy policy. The app itself does not send anything anywhere.

## What the app does not do

- It does **not** ask for any Android permissions, including internet access, so it cannot connect to the internet.
- It does **not** collect personal information, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or usage analytics.
- It does **not** show ads and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.
- It has **no** accounts, sign-in, chat or social features.
- It has **no** real-money purchases. Gems are earned only by playing and cannot be bought.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, for example by adding online features, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
